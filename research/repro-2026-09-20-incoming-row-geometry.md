Tick 35 (t=-0.000s, exactly the boundary) is the tick `mainTextLayerHidden` flips false->true and `activeLineDrawLayerHidden` flips true->false — the production swap from the whole-line dim base (`mainTextLayer`) to the bitmap single-pass active-line renderer (`NativeLyricsActiveLineDrawLayer`, `applySinglePassActiveLine`). ## Fixed / left failing

Fixed (2026-09-21, `NativeLyricsLayoutTextLayer`). The named channel was that `mainTextLayer` drew its whole-line string via CATextLayer/Core Text (CTFramesetter) while `NativeLyricsActiveLineDrawLayer` laid the SAME text out via NSLayoutManager/TextKit — two materially different engines with no documented sub-pixel rounding contract between them, so wrapped CJK lines pitched a fraction of a point apart. Rather than reconciling two engines' rounding after the fact, `mainTextLayer`/`mainBrightTextLayer` are now a CALayer subclass (`NativeLyricsLayoutTextLayer`, Sources/MusicMiniPlayerCore/UI/NativeLyricsLayerSupport.swift) that lays out and draws through the identical NSLayoutManager/NSTextContainer recipe (`lineFragmentPadding = 0`, unbounded height, word-wrap by the layer's own bounds width) and font-smoothing/subpixel-positioning context settings `NativeLyricsActiveLineDrawLayer.runImage` uses — one engine, so both rasterizers agree by construction. `NativeLyricsIncomingRowGeometryTests.test_incomingRowInkGeometryAcrossHandoff_namesTheTwitchChannel` and `test_activationDoesNotJumpTransformedInk_wrappedRows` both pass; the prior `withFixedWrapLineHeight` hand-tuned-line-height attempt (which did not move these pins at all) is left in place as a harmless no-op for single-line text.
